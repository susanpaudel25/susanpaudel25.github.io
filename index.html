About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in in Department of Applied Economics at the University of Georgia. My research focuses on time series analysis, forecasting and consumer demand. I am also interested in price transmission and agricultural economics.

My interest in these questions is shaped by master's training, prior experiences and environment.

Beyond research and academics, I like to read books, play ukulele, and hike.
